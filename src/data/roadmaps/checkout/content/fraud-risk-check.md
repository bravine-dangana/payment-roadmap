# Fraud & Risk Check

Real-time fraud detection protects merchants and customers from fraudulent transactions.

## Fraud Detection Methods

- **Velocity checks** - Too many transactions in short time
- **Device fingerprinting** - Unusual device patterns
- **Geolocation** - Payment from unusual location
- **ML models** - Machine learning anomaly detection
- **Rules engine** - Custom fraud rules

## Risk Scoring

Each transaction gets a risk score (0-100). High-risk transactions may be:
- Blocked automatically
- Sent for manual review
- Require additional verification (3DS)
