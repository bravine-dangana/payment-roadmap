# Safaricom (M-PESA) Processes Payment

Safaricom is the telecom company behind M-PESA, Kenya's dominant mobile money platform with over 30 million users.

## Processing Steps

1. Validates customer PIN
2. Checks account balance
3. Verifies transaction limits
4. Debits customer account
5. Credits float account

## M-PESA APIs

- **M-PESA Express (STK Push)** - Online payments
- **B2C** - Business to Customer payouts
- **C2B** - Customer to Business
- **B2B** - Business to Business

## Daraja API

Safaricom's Daraja API is the gateway for all M-PESA integrations.
