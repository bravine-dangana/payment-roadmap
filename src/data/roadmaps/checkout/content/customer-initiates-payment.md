# Customer Initiates Payment

The customer starts the payment journey by selecting a product or service and choosing to pay via mobile money (M-PESA).

## How it works

- Customer selects items in merchant app or website
- Chooses M-PESA as payment method
- Enters phone number registered with M-PESA
- Confirms payment amount

## Key Concepts

- **USSD** - Unstructured Supplementary Service Data, used for feature phones
- **STK Push** - SIM Toolkit push, prompts customer on smartphone
- **Payment Intent** - The initial request to start a payment
